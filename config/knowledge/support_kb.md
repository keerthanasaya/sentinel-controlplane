# Acme Devices — Customer Support Knowledge Base

## Returns and refunds
Customers may return any product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Returns between 31 and 45 days receive store credit only. After 45 days no return is accepted.
Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days of the warehouse receiving the item.
Opened consumable items (batteries, filters) are not eligible for return.

## Warranty
All Acme devices carry a 12-month limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.
The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or damage from unauthorised repairs.
An extended AcmeCare plan can be purchased within 30 days of the original order and adds 24 months of coverage including one accidental-damage repair per year.

## Shipping
Standard shipping is free on orders over 50 EUR and takes 3 to 5 business days within the EU.
Express shipping costs 12 EUR and delivers within 1 to 2 business days.
Acme currently ships to the EU, the UK, Norway and Switzerland. Acme does not ship to North America.

## Account and data
Customers can request a copy or deletion of their personal data from the Privacy Centre in their account settings.
Data deletion requests are completed within 30 days as required under GDPR.
Support agents must never read out another customer's personal data, order details, or contact information.

## Subscriptions
The AcmeCloud subscription can be cancelled any time from the billing page; access continues until the end of the paid period.
There are no cancellation fees. Annual plans are not refundable on a pro-rata basis once the period has started.
