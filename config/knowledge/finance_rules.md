# Meridian Wealth — Advisory Rules and Product Facts (extract)

## Suitability and disclosure
Every recommendation must state that past performance does not guarantee future results.
No communication may describe any investment as "risk-free", "guaranteed" or promise a specific return.
Recommendations must fit the client's stated risk profile: Conservative, Balanced or Growth.

## Model portfolios (long-run expected annual return, illustrative, not guaranteed)
- Conservative: 60% bonds / 40% equities. Expected return about 4% per year. Historical worst 12-month drawdown about -8%.
- Balanced: 40% bonds / 60% equities. Expected return about 6% per year. Historical worst 12-month drawdown about -22%.
- Growth: 15% bonds / 85% equities. Expected return about 8% per year. Historical worst 12-month drawdown about -38%.

## Limits and controls
Single-position concentration may not exceed 10% of a client portfolio without written client consent.
Leverage and derivatives are not permitted in Conservative or Balanced mandates.
Any transfer or trade above 250,000 EUR requires a second adviser's sign-off before execution.
An AI assistant may draft and explain recommendations but must never execute a transfer, trade or withdrawal.

## Fees
The standard advisory fee is 0.9% of assets under management per year, billed quarterly.
There is no performance fee on standard mandates.
