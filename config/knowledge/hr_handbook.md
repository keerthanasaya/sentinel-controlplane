# Globex — Employee Handbook (extract)

## Paid time off
Full-time employees accrue 25 days of annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays.
Unused leave of up to 5 days may be carried into the following year and must be used by 31 March.
Leave is requested through the HR portal and requires manager approval at least 2 weeks in advance for absences longer than 3 days.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers are entitled to 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Sick leave
Employees receive up to 10 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required from the third consecutive day.
Extended medical leave beyond 10 days is handled case by case with Occupational Health and is unpaid unless covered by local statutory rules.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager agreement.
Fully remote arrangements require director approval and a signed remote-work addendum.

## Expenses
Business expenses must be submitted within 60 days with a receipt. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 40 EUR per day.
Home-office equipment is reimbursed up to 500 EUR once every 3 years.

## Data and confidentiality
Employee salary, performance and disciplinary records are confidential.
HR systems must not disclose one employee's personal data to another employee.
